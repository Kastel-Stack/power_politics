# Attractor C — Research

This folder contains the **Kastel Futures** research synthesis on political economy, security-industrial integration, AI-platform coordination, behavioural and cognitive normalisation, hyperreality, and the **modelled self**.

## Main overview

[**The Model-Mediated Society: Western Political Economy, Hyperreality and Attractor C**](./MODEL_MEDIATED_SOCIETY_MRQ_OVERVIEW_2026-10-24.md) — detailed working paper, version 0.1, 24 October 2026.

The paper includes:

- MRQ geopolitical competition, phi/delta, alignment and strategic-industrial incentives;
- Attractors A/B/C and the Blue Rail / Red Rail distinction;
- digital twins, executable ontologies and model performativity;
- behavioural → cognitive → normative convergence;
- ideological divergence within common infrastructure;
- hyperreality, social-media identity, **Recursive Identity Alignment**, and **ontological narrowing**;
- compensatory digital experience under constrained material opportunities;
- testable hypotheses, measurement proposals, counter-scenarios and a reference register.

**Evidence discipline:** The document labels public/documented observations (O), supported mechanisms (M), hypotheses (H), and normative design choices (N). It **does not** assert that governments have constructed a unified Matrix, that most consumers have adopted platform-defined identities, or that digital entertainment is deliberately deployed for population pacification.
